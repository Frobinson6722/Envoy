The regular loop
What using Envoy actually looks like
Envoy is built around a repeating rhythm. Each cycle takes minutes, not hours — and the system gets better as you use it.
When you find a role (~3 min)
1
Paste into Job Feed Copy the job description from anywhere — LinkedIn, company site, a referral.
2
Envoy analyzes it AI scores fit against your profile and recommends your best contact channel — in ~60 seconds.
3
Swipe right or pass Right = enters your pipeline. Left = pass with a quick note that teaches your real filters.
For high-conviction targets (~15 min)
4
Generate a Brief Full intelligence on the company, the role, and your specific thesis. Edit it in the Drafting Room, then approve.
5
Find a warm path Envoy matches your LinkedIn network to the company and shows who you know inside.
6
Draft outreach AI writes a warm intro message. You approve it, copy it, and send it yourself.
After every conversation (~1 min)
7
Update Pipeline stage Move the opportunity forward — Screener, Hiring Manager, Offer. Add a quick note about what happened.
8
Generate Materials when ready Tailored resume and cover letter for roles you're ready to apply for. Edit, approve, then copy to submit.
Monthly (5 min)
9
Generate a Performance Digest AI analyzes your conversion rates — how many roles → screener calls → offers. Shows you what's working and what to change.
10
Adjust your Urgency Update your search urgency if timing has changed. The whole system adapts immediately.
The exchange
What Envoy asks, and what you get back
Envoy is a tool, not a service. What you put in determines what you get out.

What Envoy asks of you

One-time setup (~30 min)
Import your LinkedIn profile or fill in your background manually
Answer the Seven Dimensions — what energizes you, the culture you want, how you think about comp, and why you're looking
Upload your LinkedIn Connections.csv once to enable warm-path routing
Per role you find (~2–3 min)
Paste the job description into the Job Feed
Swipe right if interested, left to pass — leave a short note explaining why
Before anything leaves Envoy
Review and approve every AI-generated brief, resume, cover letter, and message
Copy and send manually — Envoy never sends on your behalf

Across your whole search

Monthly performance digest — conversion rates, what's working, where to focus
How Envoy is different
Three things most job tools get wrong

You approve everything

Envoy drafts — you decide. No application, message, resume, or brief goes anywhere without your explicit approval. The AI does the research and writing. You maintain complete control over what actually gets used.

Your data stays private

Your profile, preferences, and search activity are never shared with job boards, recruiters, or third parties. Envoy is a tool for you — not a lead-generation platform for employers. Your search is your business.

The system learns over time

Every pass note you leave, every preference you answer honestly, every urgency change — it all sharpens Envoy's understanding of your real filters. The analysis gets more accurate the longer you use it.
