Envoy

Your Private Career Intelligence Engine

Envoy learns what you actually want, surfaces high-conviction roles, and helps you run a disciplined, warm-path-first search — without sending your data to job boards or recruiters.

What Envoy can do
📋

Job Feed

Paste any role you find anywhere on the internet. Envoy analyzes it against your profile and tells you how to approach it — not just whether to apply.

  • Add roles from LinkedIn, company sites, referrals
  • AI scores fit and recommends contact channel
  • Swipe right = interested, left = pass
  • Leave a voice note when you pass — it teaches the system
📄

Briefs

A full intelligence brief on any role you're serious about. Know the company arc, why the role exists, and your specific thesis before you ever talk to anyone.

  • Full Brief (500–800 words) for high-conviction targets
  • Lite Brief (200 words) for early screening calls
  • Authenticity score keeps language in your voice
  • You approve before anything leaves Envoy
📝

Materials

Auto-generate a tailored resume and cover letter for each opportunity, built from your profile and the specific role — not a generic template.

  • Tailored resume reordered for each role
  • Cover letter in your voice, not AI-speak
  • Edit in the Drafting Room before approving
  • Authenticity score flags anything that sounds off
🤝

Network

Import your LinkedIn connections once. For any target company, instantly see who you know — and draft a warm outreach message to the right person.

  • Upload Connections.csv from LinkedIn export
  • Find internal connections at any target company
  • AI drafts a warm intro message
  • You approve and send manually — no auto-send
📊

Pipeline

Track every opportunity from first look to offer. See your funnel, spot where deals stall, and get a monthly AI digest on what to double down on.

  • 8-stage funnel: Swiped Right → Offer
  • Brief and materials status at a glance
  • Move stages with one click + a quick note
  • Monthly performance digest with insights
🧠

Profile & Preferences

The foundation everything else builds on. The Seven Dimensions interview teaches Envoy what you actually want — not just your skills, but what energizes you.

  • Import from LinkedIn CSV (or fill manually)
  • Seven Dimensions: energy, culture, management, team, trajectory, comp, timing
  • Urgency setting changes how the whole system behaves
  • Update any time — system adapts immediately

How to use Envoy — the workflow

1

Complete your profile setup (this onboarding)

Fill in your background, answer the Seven Dimensions honestly, and set your urgency level. This is the intelligence layer — everything else runs on it. Budget ~30 minutes the first time.

Start now → click "1. Profile" above
2

Import your LinkedIn connections

Go to the Network page and upload your Connections.csv. This unlocks warm-path routing for every role you add. You only do this once.

LinkedIn → Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data
3

Add your first 3–5 roles to the Job Feed

Paste in any role you've seen or heard about — LinkedIn, company sites, referrals, anything. Envoy will analyze each one and show you a fit thesis + recommended contact channel within about 60 seconds.

Job Feed → Add a Role → paste the full job description
4

Swipe on each role

Review the AI's fit analysis. Swipe right if you're interested (it enters your Pipeline). Swipe left to pass — leave a short note explaining why, so the system learns your real filters over time.

Keyboard shortcut: ← Pass  |  → Interested
5

Generate a Brief for your top 2–3 targets

Go to Briefs and generate a Full Brief for any role you're serious about. Read it, edit it in the Drafting Room, and approve it. This becomes the backbone of every outreach and conversation.

Briefs → select role → Generate Brief → edit → Approve
6

Find a warm path and draft your outreach

On the Network page, select a target company and click "Find Connections." If you have someone inside, select them and draft a warm message. You copy and send it manually — Envoy never sends anything on your behalf.

Network → Find Connections → Draft Message → Copy → Send yourself
7

Generate tailored materials when ready to apply

Go to Materials, select the role, and generate a tailored resume and cover letter. Review the authenticity score, edit anything that sounds off, then approve. Keep them in Envoy until you need to submit.

Materials → select role → Generate → edit → Approve → Copy
8

Track everything in Pipeline

After each interaction — a reply, a screener call, an interview — go to Pipeline and move the stage forward. Add a note about what happened. At month-end, generate a Performance Digest to see your conversion rates and where to focus.

Pipeline → Move → update stage + note → end of month: Generate Digest
